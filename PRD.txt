1. TL;DR
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Ngepos is a modern, mobile-first web-based Point of Sale (POS) system designed
for small to medium-sized food & beverage businesses in Indonesia. Built with
SolidStart (SPA mode) + Dexie.js (IndexedDB), it runs entirely in the browser
with offline-first architecture — no server required for core operations.

The system provides instant checkout, advanced product management with variant
& recipe support, COGS/HPP tracking with margin analytics, multi-channel
payment processing (Cash, QRIS, GoFood, GrabFood, ShopeeFood), expense
tracking, financial reporting, and role-based staff management.

Target Users : Restaurant owners, café operators, food stall vendors, and
               small retail businesses requiring a flexible, mobile-accessible
               POS solution.

Why It Matters: Most existing POS systems are either too complex, server-
               dependent, or lack the flexibility needed by modern mobile-
               first businesses. Ngepos bridges that gap with an intuitive
               interface, offline-first architecture, and built-in business
               intelligence for F&B operations.


2. GOALS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Business Goals:
  • Capture market share in the mobile-first POS segment for Indonesian F&B
  • Achieve <15 second average checkout time through optimized mobile UI
  • Drive adoption through zero-setup onboarding (no server, no registration)
  • Enable data-driven decisions via built-in margin analytics and reporting
  • Build a scalable, offline-first platform supporting online & offline modes

User Goals:
  • Complete transactions in under 30 seconds with minimal taps
  • Access the POS from any device without app installation (PWA)
  • Manage inventory with automatic cost tracking and margin visibility
  • Process payments through multiple channels (Cash, QRIS, delivery platforms)
  • Generate reports for sales, expenses, profit margins, and operational data
  • Record past transactions to maintain complete historical records
  • Operate without internet connectivity disruptions

Non-Goals (Current Phase):
  • Building native iOS or Android applications
  • Complex enterprise ERP integrations
  • Built-in accounting software (future integration target)
  • Supplier management and procurement workflows
  • Customer loyalty program management
  • Multi-location franchise operations

5. USER EXPERIENCE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Primary Journey — Completing a Sale: [IMPLEMENTED]
  1. User opens app → sees product grid with category tabs and search
  2. Taps product → adds to cart (or opens variant selector if variants exist)
  3. Floating cart button shows item count and total in real-time
  4. Cart sheet slides up → full item list, edit variants, adjust quantities
  5. Taps "Bayar" → payment method selection (Cash/QRIS/GoFood/etc.)
  6. For QRIS: static QR shown; for Cash: amount received → change calculated
  7. For Platform: amount adjustment for mark-up/discount recorded
  8. Transaction saved → receipt generated → redirect to receipt page

Secondary Journey — Recording a Past Transaction: [IMPLEMENTED]
  1. User navigates to History → taps "Catat Penjualan Lampau"
  2. Date/time picker allows selecting when sale occurred
  3. Standard product selection flow follows
  4. Transaction tagged as backdated with visual indicator in history

Variant Selection Flow: [IMPLEMENTED]
  1. Product with variants shows "Kustom" badge in catalog grid
  2. Tapping product opens VariantSelector sheet (reusable component)
  3. User selects from SINGLE or MULTIPLE variant groups
  4. Price adjusts in real-time based on selected modifiers
  5. Cart shows variants inline; edit button allows re-selection from cart

Margin Analytics Flow: [IMPLEMENTED]
  1. In product create/edit → "RESEP & HPP" tab
  2. Add raw materials with qty, unit, cost per unit
  3. Margin indicator auto-computes: profit, %, and status tier
  4. 4-tier color system: Kritis(<30%), Tipis(30-44%), Sehat(45-71%), Optimal(≥72%)
  5. Toggleable educational guide explains each tier
  6. Product list shows margin badge for products with HPP data

Navigation Structure (Bottom Tab Bar):
  • Kasir    — POS / product grid, checkout
  • Riwayat  — Transaction history with date filtering
  • Pengeluaran — Expense tracking and categorization
  • Laporan  — Financial reports (Omset, HPP, Profit)
  • Setelan  — Settings hub (outlet, products, categories, payment, staff, roles)
